The ‘sensitive plant’ (mimosa pudica) is named for its unusual defense against herbivores. When touched, the plant folds all its leaves against its stem. 

{% include image.html image="projects/plant/Mimosa_Pudica.gif" %}

The ‘Scaredy Plant’ was the fruit of a semester-long sophomore class at Olin called ‘Principals of Engineering’. Essentially we were placed in teams of around four and tasked with planning and executing a project with mechanical, electrical, and software systems. 

My team decided to make a robotic house plant with anxiety. 

{% include image.html image="projects/plant/plant2.jpg" %}

My team was three software engineers and me. At the beginning of the class, I wasn’t sure I was ready to be the mechanical engineering lead on anything, but after long weeks of SolidWorks, tinkering, and hours in the laser cutter room we had a working robot. 

{% include youtubePlayer.html id=page.youtubeId %}

##Construction
The leaves are laminated paper, laser cut to create the fold structure. Fishing line runs down the center and through the stem to a servo.
